# Q'd

**Saved from your feed. Judged by us.**

Q'd (said "queued") saves the movie and TV recommendations you find on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Then it judges you for never watching them.

## Watch the 60-second tour

▶️ [Watch the Q'd feature video](media/qd-features-60s.mp4) (56 seconds, vertical)

## What it does

- **Save from any short video.** Upload a screenshot or paste a caption, and AI finds the movie or show.
- **One list with real posters.** Filter it by genre, length or what you've watched.
- **Where to watch.** See which service is streaming it.
- **Roast my watchlist.** Judge Flix tells you what it thinks of your list.
- **Group chat shaming.** A pretend "intervention" text to your friends.
- **Match me.** Swipe through picks based on what you've saved.
- **Decision Court.** Two people can't agree, so Judge Flix picks. The loser plays Popcorn Panic.
